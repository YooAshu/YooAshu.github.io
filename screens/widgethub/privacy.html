Privacy Policy

WidgetHub respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the WidgetHub mobile application ("the App"), developed by Genix Apps.

1. Information We Collect

WidgetHub does not require account registration or sign-in. We do not operate servers that store your personal information. Everything you configure in the App — such as widget settings, favorites, quotes, colors, and photos you choose for photo widgets — is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to us.

2. Permissions and How They Are Used

The App requests certain permissions only to power specific widget features. Each permission is optional unless the related feature is used:

  • Notifications: used to show the notification of the real-time widget sync service and any alerts you enable.
  • Foreground service (real-time widget sync): Android limits how often home screen widgets can refresh, and some device manufacturers also stop background apps to save battery. This can make clock and battery widgets lag or freeze. If you turn on real-time widget sync in Settings, the App runs a foreground service that listens for system events (such as the time changing or the battery level changing) so these widgets stay accurate. Android requires a persistent notification while this service runs. The service only updates your widgets on your device. It does not collect, store, or transmit any data, and it is off unless you enable it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, after which widgets return to Android's normal update schedule and may update less often.
  • Location: used only by widgets that need your location, and only when you grant the permission. Location is processed on your device for the widget's purpose and is not stored by us or linked to your identity.
  • Notification access (media playback): used only by the Music widgets to read the currently playing track's title, artist, artwork, and playback state, and to show playback controls. This information is processed on your device, is displayed only in your widget, and is never stored, transmitted, or shared. Notification content other than media playback information is not read or retained.
  • Photos / media: used only when you choose a photo for a photo widget or quote background. Selected images are copied into the App's private storage on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Battery information: used to display battery level and charging status in battery widgets. This stays on your device.

3. No Advertisements

WidgetHub does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising SDKs. No user data is shared with advertisers or marketing firms.

4. Premium Purchases and Subscriptions

Users may purchase a premium upgrade through Google Play Billing, offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, and may be eligible for a free trial. All payments are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment or billing information. We only receive confirmation from Google Play of whether premium access is active. Purchases and subscriptions are subject to Google Play's terms of service, and you can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Google Play account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. Third-party services integrated in the App — namely Google Play Services and Google Play Billing — operate under their own independent privacy policies. The content of your widgets, photos, and quotes is not shared with these services.

6. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data, including deletion by removing widgets, clearing App data, or uninstalling the App from your device settings. We do not maintain any remote servers or databases containing your personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

WidgetHub is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable regulations. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
